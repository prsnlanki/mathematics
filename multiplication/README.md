# Multiplication

`Mathematics::Multiplication`: the tables 2–20 × 2–20, 865 notes generated by `build.py` (there is no source data to edit).

| Card | Notes | Front → back | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | 361 | `7 × 8` → `56` | typed; both orders are separate cards |
| Division | 361 | `56 ÷ 7` → `8` | typed; one per ordered pair |
| Factoring | 143 | `36 = ? × ?` (4 pairs) → `2 × 18`, `3 × 12`, `4 × 9`, `6 × 6` | self-graded: say every pair in your head, then flip |

**Timer.** Product and division cards show the seconds since the card appeared; the back shows the time it took, to a tenth of a second. No limit and no colours. It's a small script in the card template that works on Anki desktop (one page for both sides) and AnkiDroid/AnkiMobile (the back is a new page; the start time is kept in `sessionStorage`).

**New-card order.** `order()` in `build.py` sets each note's position, which Anki keeps on import:

1. Products easiest first, by band: the 2–10 table, then ×11, ×12, ×15 and ×20, then anything with 13, 14, 16, 17, 18 or 19. Within a band by the larger factor, then the smaller, with `a × b` right before `b × a`.
2. Each division card 30 products after its product (`DIVISION_DELAY`): about 3 days later at 20 new cards a day.
3. A product's factoring card right after the last division card that gives it.

Leave Anki's deck options at **Gather order: Deck** and **Sort order: Card type, then order gathered** (the defaults) or **Order gathered**; a random sort order undoes this.

**Tags.** `kind::product`, `kind::division`, `kind::factoring`; typed cards also `band::1`–`band::3`.

**Changing it.** Edit `build.py`; `build()` checks the counts, that every division follows its product and every factoring card its divisions, and that GUIDs are unique. GUIDs come from the kind and the numbers (`guid_for("mathematics", "multiplication", kind, a, b)`), so keep them when changing a card's wording, or re-importing duplicates it.
