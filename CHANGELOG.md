# Changelog

Each version is a release tagged `vx.y.z` on the [releases page](https://github.com/prsnlanki/mathematics/releases). Major: importing it needs a step in Anki. Minor: new cards. Patch: fixes.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-19)

- First release: 865 notes in `Mathematics::Multiplication`, the tables 2–20 × 2–20. **Products** (361, both orders: 7 × 8 and 8 × 7) and **divisions** (361: 56 ÷ 7) to type, with a timer showing the seconds since the card appeared; **factoring** (143: 36 → 2 × 18, 3 × 12, 4 × 9, 6 × 6), self-graded, with the number of pairs as a cue.
- New cards come easiest first: the 2–10 table, then ×11, ×12, ×15 and ×20, then the facts with 13, 14, 16, 17, 18 or 19. A division card comes about 3 days after its product card (at 20 new cards a day), and a product's factoring card right after the last division that gives it.
